# Example data and provenance

Both files are **self-created and synthetic**. Neither comes from ANSYS or from a CAD vendor.
They were generated by `tools/gen_examples.cpp` (run `gen_examples data`). No third-party data is used, so no licence applies beyond the project's own.

## `example_rod.stl`
- Format: **ASCII STL**, one closed solid (`example_rod`), 96 triangles.
- Geometry: cylinder along +x, length 1.0, radius 0.0056419 (cross-section about 1e-4 in squared units), 24 segments, end caps at x=0 and x=1.
- Units: STL has no unit field. The model coordinates are treated as **metres** here, and the UI labels them "model units".

## `example_nodal_temperature_SYNTHETIC.csv`
- **SYNTHETIC. Not an actual ANSYS export.** It follows the schema of an ANSYS nodal temperature export so the importer can be tested.
- Schema: `node_id,x_m,y_m,z_m,temperature_C`; coordinates in **metres**, temperature in **degrees Celsius**.
- Lines starting with `#` are comments (the parser ignores them; real exports may not contain them).
- 273 nodes: 21 stations along x, each with 1 axis node and 12 ring nodes at the rod radius.
- Temperature: the analytical rod solution T(x) = 100 - 80x + 500x(1-x), which peaks near 188 degC at x about 0.4 (from Task C), plus +0.5 degC on the axis nodes as a made-up radial rise.

## Tested limits
Default limits are 200 MB and 2,000,000 triangles (STL) or 5,000,000 rows (CSV). Measured limits are reported in `docs/` once benchmarked.
