1D steady-state rod (linear FE)

Solves −d/dx(kA dT/dx) = qA with T(0)=T₀ and T(L)=TL. Lateral surfaces insulated, constant properties.

Mesh convergence

Nh [m]Max nodal error [°C]Max midpoint error [°C]Observed order

Midpoint error uses the FE interpolation (average of the two nodal values) against Texact(x) = T₀ + (TL−T₀)x/L + q/(2k)·x(L−x). Nodal errors are at round-off level because the exact solution is quadratic; the midpoint error shrinks as h².

Assumptions

  • Units: m, m², W/(m·K), W/m³, °C. Constant k and A; no heat loss through the lateral surface.
  • Two-node elements: element stiffness (kA/h)[[1,−1],[−1,1]] and consistent load qAh/2 per node.
  • Dirichlet temperatures are imposed by moving the known nodal values to the right-hand side of the neighbouring rows and replacing the constrained rows by identity rows.

Serial vs CPU-parallel batch of thermal solves

Protocol: 1 warm-up and 5 measured repetitions per worker count; the median is reported. Only the solve step is timed. Cancelling discards the partial run, so no half-finished numbers are shown.
